CTFs Walkthrough

A Cybersecurity challenge, known as a Capture the Flag (CTF), involves participants searching for and exploiting vulnerabilities on target machines to access sensitive data, or "flags". To succeed, participants must utilise their cybersecurity expertise in different domains of Cybersecurity such as web exploitation, binary exploitation, cryptography, forensics, networking, OSINT, etc.
